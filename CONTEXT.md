# Physical construction design

Stud composes physical parts and their interfaces into designs, with separate
evidence for geometry, fabrication and construction choices.

## Language

**Bearing interface**: A named supporting surface and its declared seat location,
width and elevation. Its actual contact is measurable independently of the declaration.
_Avoid_: Support point when a surface is intended.

**Member profile**: The actual cross section and purchasing basis of stock used
to cut framing members, including solid and factory I sections.

**Framing region**: A floor area with a common joist direction, elevation and
support scheme. A building can contain several composed regions.

**Opening**: One named clear void shared by displaced framing and covering cuts.
_Avoid_: Separate stair-hole dimensions in each construction operation.

**Connection detail**: The chosen physical interface, hardware and fastening
requirements between named parts. Contact alone is geometric evidence.

**Design basis**: Sourced selections, accepted assumptions and unresolved inputs
used to choose construction. A drawing is one possible source.

**Geometric evidence**: Native measurements of declared relationships and
expected part coverage. It is distinct from structural sizing and approval.
